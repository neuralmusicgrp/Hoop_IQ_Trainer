Hoop IQ Trainer — Privacy & Usage Analytics

Effective October 16, 2026 · Anthony Boyd / Worldmedia Publishing & Consulting LLC

What the app stores on your device

Hoop IQ Trainer is a public educational website. It stores quiz progress, optional player name, role mode, and coach-edited terminology locally in your browser using localStorage. These records are not automatically uploaded to our servers. Exported files remain under your control until you choose to share them. Clearing browser storage may erase locally saved progress or custom edits.

Optional Google Analytics 4

Google Analytics is off by default. Only if you select “Allow usage analytics” does the site load Google's measurement tag (property ID G-ETJG7F3CFD). If enabled, it can collect app page visits, study and diagram sessions started, quiz sessions started and completed, score percentage and question count, and whether the visitor initiated feedback or opened the coach review form.

The app's custom analytics events do not include player names, email addresses, individual quiz answers, edited coaching notes, school names, or other user-entered text. The page-view URL we send omits query parameters and fragments. Google may still process information associated with the visitor's device and network, including cookies, device/browser information and approximate location, under Google's services and policies.

Analytics is used to understand interest in lessons and improve usability. We do not turn on Google Ads personalization through this code. Google Analytics data retention is subject to the settings in the Google Analytics property.

Your choice

The home screen asks for permission when no choice has been saved. Choosing “No thanks” keeps our Google Analytics tag unloaded. You can change your choice anytime in Player / Settings → Analytics & Privacy. Turning analytics off stops further app-generated events, requests denied consent and attempts to delete this property's analytics cookies; it cannot retract data already received by Google.

Because students may use this app, visitors under 18 should ask a parent or guardian to decide whether analytics may be enabled on their device. The app does not ask for students' personal details for analytics.

Feedback and third-party links

Feedback sent by email goes through your selected email service only after you send it. The optional coach review form opens a Jotform form hosted by a third party, subject to its own privacy practices. Short.io promotional links can report link-click statistics separately from the site's optional Google Analytics.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · Jotform Privacy Policy.
