How to Use Hoop IQ Trainer

Quick Start

Use this flow when you are new to the app, helping a player study, or checking whether the language matches how your team teaches.

  1. 1. Start in StudyRead each basketball term, teaching cue, common mistake, and diagram explanation.
  2. 2. Use Diagram StudyLook at the court diagram and connect the player movement to the definition.
  3. 3. Take the QuizTest yourself on the terms. Missed answers show what you need to study more.
  4. 4. Use Name This ActionLook at the diagram, then choose the basketball action.
  5. 5. Review WeaknessesGo back to the terms you missed and study them again.
  6. 6. Use Team FeedbackPlayers, coaches, or parents can send feedback, corrections, or suggestions for better team language from Coach & App Tools.
  7. 7. Coach ToolsCoaches can adjust wording so the app matches how the team teaches each action on this device.
