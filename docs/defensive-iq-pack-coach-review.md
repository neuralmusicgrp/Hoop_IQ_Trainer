# Defensive IQ Pack — Coach Review Draft

**Approval status: PENDING. Neither public v3 nor private v4 is ready to merge or release this content.**
Baseline: v0.9.15; 50 default terms. Proposal: 16 additions + 11 existing defensive revisions = 66 total terms, 27 defensive entries.
Original request: five existing and sixteen new concepts (21); six other already-present defensive coverages also receive defender-oriented cue updates so the complete pack is consistent.

## Review guidance
- Verify all player descriptions, coach language, mistake corrections, and scheme dependencies against your team's playbook.
- Shell is classified as a teaching drill, not a separate universal defensive scheme.
- Distinguish no-middle forcing rules from pack-line help principles; teams may use different sideline/baseline guidance.
- Communication Calls remains an umbrella term unless the coach directs the creation of individual call terms.
- Sixteen new static diagrams are distinct and their coordinates pass programmatic checks, but their basketball accuracy and readability require human sign-off.
- Static defensive diagrams: numbered markers = offensive players; X-prefixed markers = defenders; black arrows = movement; dashed blue = passes.
- Representative four-defender scenes (e.g., Communication Calls) are teaching snapshots, not complete 5-on-5 alignments. Where a responsibility is not depicted, the diagram instruction now calls that out explicitly.
- Saved user customizations remain protected: only text identical to prior factory defaults migrates to revised teaching language.
- Full review includes answer-key clarity, mobile readability, and visual consistency, not just syntax and term count.

### Low Man — Existing revision (`low-man`)
**Category:** Defensive Concept
**Player definition:** The deepest weak-side help defender nearest the basket, responsible for protecting the rim and often delaying a rolling screener before the team rotates.
**Coach proposal:** PROPOSED — coach review required: Identify the low man early, tag the roller when required by our scheme, then call the next rotation and recover or X-out on the pass.
**Defender cue:** Call 'low!' or the team's agreed help call; protect the rim, then find the next matchup.
**Common mistake:** Watching the ball while the roller slips behind, or tagging with no planned recovery.
**Diagram instruction:** X3 starts on the weak-side baseline, steps into the roller's path and communicates coverage behind the ball screen.
**Scenario:** The roller breaks free down the lane. Which defender is commonly responsible for the first weak-side tag?
**Intended answer:** The low man, subject to the team's help rules.
**Other choices:** The ball handler's primary on-ball defender. | The screener's defender nearest half court. | The first defender who calls a timeout.

### Drop — Existing revision (`drop`)
**Category:** Defensive Coverage
**Player definition:** A ball-screen coverage in which the screener's defender retreats to protect the basket and the on-ball defender fights over or through the screen to recover.
**Coach proposal:** PROPOSED — coach review required: Keep the ball between the two defenders. The big communicates depth; the guard reconnects from behind without conceding a layup.
**Defender cue:** Big: call 'drop'; contain the drive and roller. Guard: fight through and recover to the ball.
**Common mistake:** The big backs all the way to the rim without addressing the ball, or the guard dies on the screen.
**Diagram instruction:** X5 retreats in the lane while X1 chases the ball handler over the screen; they protect the rim and pocket pass together.
**Scenario:** What must the on-ball defender do when their teammate is playing drop coverage?
**Intended answer:** Navigate the screen and recover to the ball handler.
**Other choices:** Immediately abandon the handler and guard the corner. | Switch automatically without calling it. | Double-team the ball alone after the screen.

### Shell Defense / Shell Drill — New (`shell-defense`)
**Category:** Defensive Drill
**Player definition:** A team-defense teaching drill in which players shift between guarding the ball, denying a pass, and helping away from the ball as passes move around the perimeter.
**Coach proposal:** PROPOSED — coach review required: In a 4-on-4 shell, freeze after each pass to check ball-you-player vision, help gaps, low-man positioning and controlled closeouts; add live drives gradually.
**Defender cue:** Call 'ball', 'deny' and 'help'; move on the pass, not after the catch.
**Common mistake:** Standing still off the ball or losing sight of the assigned player during a rotation.
**Diagram instruction:** Four offensive spots on the perimeter with four matching defenders: X1 pressures the ball, X2 is a pass away, X3 and X4 are in help positions.
**Scenario:** The ball moves from the top to the wing during shell drill. When should off-ball defenders adjust?
**Intended answer:** While the pass is moving, before the catch.
**Other choices:** Only when the passer has taken their next dribble. | After the receiver already starts driving. | Only after a coach yells stop.

### Closeout — New (`closeout`)
**Category:** Defensive Fundamental
**Player definition:** The controlled approach a defender uses to reach a shooter or receiver, contest a shot and stay balanced against a drive.

### Scram Switch — New (`scram-switch`)
**Category:** Defensive Concept
**Player definition:** An off-ball switch used to remove an unfavorable defender, often a smaller player, from a post mismatch before the offense can exploit it.
**Coach proposal:** PROPOSED — coach review required: A bigger teammate calls the scram, replaces the small defender on the post player and sends the small defender to the new matchup before the entry pass.
**Defender cue:** Call 'scram' early; swap out of a bad post mismatch.

**Intended answer:** To remove an unfavorable mismatch, especially a small defender guarding the post.
